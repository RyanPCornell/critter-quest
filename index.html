❓ How to play

  • Walk with ←↑→↓ or WASD — or just click where you want to go.
  • Wild critters and 🔮 loose orbs pop up in wild patches. Click a critter bubble to chase it; click a lone orb to grab it instantly. Some critters guard an orb you also get if you catch them.
  • Each region has its own critters — explore the meadow, woods, lake, volcanic ridge, desert, snowy tundra, glowing marsh, and crystal caverns. Seven legendary critters roam everywhere...
  • Orbs catch critters. You throw an orb from a critter's home region (see your 🔮 Orbs bag). Find them in the wild, earn them from catches, and get a batch every level-up. Prism Orbs catch legendaries and work on anything in a pinch.
  • Evolve your critters! Feed a critter enough orbs of its region (shown on its Critterdex page) to grow it into a stronger form.
  • Add classmates by trainer name in 🤝 Friends, then trade critters with them. You can only offer a critter you have two or more of, so your Critterdex stays complete.
  • In an encounter, answer math problems or spell words to weaken the critter's will and throw your orb. The easiest spelling mode shows a picture and you fill in the missing letters!
  • Miss three times and it flees! Rarer critters resist more throws — and legendaries carry an ⚡ aura guard that takes extra correct answers to crack before any orb can fly.
  • Make the trainer yours: pick your hat, hair, skin, shirt, and pants in ⚙️ Settings → My Avatar.
  • Fill your 📖 Critterdex — catching a critter reveals its stats, powers, and story.
  • Chat with the townsfolk — each person on the map now has a floating 💬 name banner so they're easy to spot. Solve their math problem for a clue to where an ✦ Ultra Legendary is hiding. Ultra Legendaries (like Sergio the Maine Coon!) wait in one spot until you find them — catch one and a new one appears somewhere new.
  • Step onto the ✦ Astral Portal near the village to travel to a secret portal-only region — a starry rift full of rare and legendary critters! A Portal Home takes you back.
  • Visit 🛒 Shops to trade orbs for other orb types or buy an Ultra Compass, and ⚔️ Arenas to battle the critter left guarding it — pick your champion, answer to attack, and win to leave your own critter on guard.
  • On a phone or iPad, use the on-screen ⬍ D-pad (or just tap where you want to go).
